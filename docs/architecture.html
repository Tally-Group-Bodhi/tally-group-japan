What it's built with.

Modern, mainstream tools — picked for speed, longevity, and so any agency or in-house team can pick the project up. No exotic dependencies, no vendor lock-in.

Hosting
Where the site lives
A globally-distributed network that serves the site from the closest server to each visitor — fast for both Australian and overseas users.
Vercel / Netlify ready

02

How it all fits together.

Every visitor request flows through the same path. The site is mostly pre-built (which is why it's fast), with a few pieces that can be edited live by the marketing team.

V
Visitor
A potential customer, journalist, or candidate visits the site.
⚡
Global CDN
Edge servers around the world deliver the page in milliseconds.
N
Next.js
The site itself — pages, layouts, components.
S
Sanity CMS
Pulls live blog posts, careers, testimonials.
M
Marketing team
Edits content directly through the Sanity Studio dashboard.

Pre-built pages

Most pages (home, products, about) are built once at deploy time and served as plain HTML — that's why first-load is sub-second.

Live content

Blog posts, careers and testimonials pull from the CMS so marketing can update them at any time without redeploying the site.

Forms & interactions

Things like the demo-request form and region selector run in the browser, with all data validated before submission.

SEO & sitemaps

Auto-generated sitemap.xml and robots.txt mean Google indexes new content within hours of publishing.

Clear separation between content (marketing edits in CMS) and structure (developers update in code). The split keeps the marketing team unblocked while keeping the design system consistent.

05

Why this matters.

Six properties of the build that translate directly into business outcomes — speed, search visibility, brand consistency, and the ability to move fast.

F · 01

Sub-second pages

Pre-built HTML and an edge network mean visitors see the homepage in roughly 250 ms — about 4× faster than the typical corporate site. Faster pages = higher conversion.

F · 02

SEO-ready out of the box

Auto-generated sitemap, robots file, structured metadata on every page, and clean URLs. New content is indexed by Google within hours, not weeks.

F · 03

Mobile-first responsive

Every page is designed mobile-up, then progressively enhanced for tablet and desktop. Tested across phones, tablets, and ultra-wide monitors.

F · 04

Marketing team independence

Blog posts, careers, and testimonials all editable in a clean dashboard. No more "raise a ticket to fix a typo." Engineering only gets called for new functionality.

F · 05

One brand, two regions

Australian and US corporate sites share the same components and data model, but each can tailor copy, leadership, and contact details independently.

F · 06

No vendor lock-in

Built on open-source standards (React, Next.js, Tailwind). Any modern web team — agency or in-house — can pick up the project tomorrow without retraining.
